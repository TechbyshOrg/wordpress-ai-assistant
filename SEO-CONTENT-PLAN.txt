======================================================================
1. POSITIONING
======================================================================

One-sentence definition (use everywhere):
  A free AI writing assistant for WordPress that writes WooCommerce product
  descriptions, SEO titles and meta descriptions, and image alt text, using your
  own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, Groq, or OpenRouter API key.

Differentiators (all verified in code):
  - Product copy + SEO meta + alt text in one plugin.
  - 6 text providers with your own key; no subscription, no Pro upsell.
  - Free Bulk Fill (up to 20 products) that never overwrites existing text.
  - Writes into Yoast SEO, Rank Math, AIOSEO, and SEOPress.
  - Alt text written from the image itself (vision models).
  - Auto SEO meta on publish, in the background.
  - ACF, Meta Box, Elementor, WPML, Polylang support.
  - Loads nothing on the public site.
